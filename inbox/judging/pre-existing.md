# Pre-existing components

Declaration for the form (rules section 5: work before the build window opened on 22 October 00:00 UTC must be declared).

- Paul's Star Wars gift finder, at the repo root (`src/`, `data/`, `prompts/`, `run.sh`). A practice build made before the challenge was announced. It is not part of the submission; `inbox/` does not use it.
- Kristof's data pack, `kristof/data`, committed on 22 October inside the build window. It is the seed of the synthetic data: `inbox/data/synthetic` is generated by `inbox/scripts/gen_company.py`, and its first 24 thread lines are byte-identical to `kristof/data/threads/threads.jsonl` (per [zetaris-register.md](zetaris-register.md)).
- The Meterless Scout Intent evals, copied into `inbox/engines/scout-evals/` at commit `1742dec` of github.com/Meterless/Meterless, Apache-2.0 (see [../engines/scout-evals/NOTICE-SOURCE.md](../engines/scout-evals/NOTICE-SOURCE.md)).
- Open-source development tools: tsx (MIT), yaml (ISC), esbuild (MIT, transitive), pyarrow (Apache-2.0, used only to generate the Parquet file).

Nothing else is pre-existing. The Scout and H-MEM engines in `inbox/src/engines/` are our own implementation of the Meterless specs, not a copy.
