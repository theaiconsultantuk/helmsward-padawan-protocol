Version 2, 60 s, the same story with more of the product and more of the cast (Kristof, 23 Oct: at least twice as long, human-facing text held longer):

v2 adds the synthetic data in its seven formats flowing into Zetaris and the Unified Semantic Layer, the front page's three doors, the dashboard where the backup affirms the held draft and the trust ledger moves, Vader's desk, and the loop as a ring of its ten trace steps with the sponsor behind each. Every message, card and caption holds at least three seconds.

The story: Cass Holm asks for 40 USD on 22 Oct, under the limit; the refund category sits under the trust floor, so the draft is held, and the owner (Soren Adey) is on holiday with the backup (Wynn Okonkwo) named. On 27 Oct she asks for 400 USD, over the limit; Risk refuses the draft and the ticket goes to the over-limit refund owner (Caleb Orth) with the draft attached and the first request read from H-MEM. Every amount, name and branch line on screen is copied from that run. One composition choice to know: the escalation card's line "Earlier request from this customer" summarises the run's `memory.read` trace line; the queue packet the approver receives carries the ticket, the draft and both critiques, and the loop reads the memory before planning rather than copying it into the packet.

Cast, for the fun of it: Padmé as the customer, Admiral Ackbar's shadow as the owner who has gone fishing, Chewbacca as the backup, Darth Vader as the refund owner. The character images were supplied by Kristof; the Jedi Pack art is the team's. Music: "RoninCadence" by DiuleJ, from his DMCA-free library. Sound effects: CC0 (Kenney). The compositions themselves live outside this repo; only the renders and posters are committed.
