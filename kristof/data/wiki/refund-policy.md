# Refund policy

Fictional policy for Jedi Pack Inc. The company is not real.

A refund at or under 150.00 USD can be approved by employee 15. Their backup is employee 16.
A refund above 150.00 USD goes to the manager, employee 3. Their backup is employee 4.

The amount is compared with this page's limit. The model does not choose the limit.
A replacement is not a refund. Replacement requests use the replacement row in the responsibility table.
