# Brag videos

Two 23 s cuts of the same storyboard, made with Hyperframes from the shipped front page, the trace lines and the names in [refund-chain.md](../refund-chain.md) (a live run):

- `brag-landscape.mp4` (1920x1080) with its poster `brag-landscape.jpg`
- `brag-vertical.mp4` (1080x1920) with its poster `brag-vertical.jpg`

The story: Cass Holm asks for 40 USD on 22 Oct, under the limit; the refund category sits under the trust floor, so the draft is held, and the owner (Soren Adey) is on holiday with the backup (Wynn Okonkwo) named. On 27 Oct she asks for 400 USD, over the limit; Risk refuses the draft and the ticket goes to the over-limit refund owner (Caleb Orth) with the draft attached and the first request read from H-MEM. Every amount, name and branch line on screen is copied from that run.

Cast, for the fun of it: Padmé as the customer, Admiral Ackbar's shadow as the owner who has gone fishing, Chewbacca as the backup, Darth Vader as the refund owner. The character images were supplied by Kristof; the Jedi Pack art is the team's. Music: "RoninCadence" by DiuleJ, from his DMCA-free library. Sound effects: CC0 (Kenney). The compositions themselves live outside this repo; only the renders and posters are committed.
