# Where the points are

Source: ClaudeBWAI's review of the Scout verdict, from `plans/jedi-pack-inbox-build.md`. The Scout verdict gave the scoped cut a floor of 80/100 (Impact 23, Technical 16, Innovation 11, Demo 13, Product & UX 8, Sponsor 9). The Estimate column is the planner's estimate if every lever lands (86-88 in total). It is an estimate, not a score. The Landed column is checked against the repo as of CP5a (23 Oct 2026); the Sponsor and Product & UX rows were refreshed for the scenarios slice (World Model, scenario proofs in [scenarios.md](scenarios.md), the two pages, the journey).
