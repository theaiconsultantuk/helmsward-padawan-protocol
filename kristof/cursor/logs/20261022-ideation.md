## Locked decisions

- One track. Track 3, Reasoning Architecture. Fit 9/10. Track 2 is the evidence-memo tilt at 7/10. Track 1 is 4/10. Bonus Tinkerer is 1/10.
- One sentence: the agent helps a Jedi Pack Inc mailbox owner decide send, hold, or escalate by joining holiday, policy, responsibility, and backup in Zetaris with a trust floor, and produces a cited reply or a human ticket.
- The company is fictional. The name collides with the HackOS team name. The brief says so.
- BACKUP-1 is dropped. Do not spawn it.
- Items 15, 17, and 18 are one loop. Correct means the Evaluator passes and Risk signs the same draft. A negative round is either failing. The escalate gate fires at 2 negative rounds, or immediately when a hard fact another draft cannot change (amount, may_leave, or both owner and backup out). No third draft. No randomizer training loop. A customer saying "this is wrong" starts a new loop on the same ticket.
- Evaluator is Nightshift's judge lane. NVIDIA Nemotron writes the critique. POWER is Nightshift. NVIDIA is the model inside it.
- Risk is Scout's risk check, the policy gate. It is code, not a model. It is not Gaia Oracle.
- Escalate is H-MEM `queueForHuman`. Auto-resolve only at decision confidence 0.70 or above. Below that, queue for a human and do not guess. Both Risk and the Evaluator write feedback into the ticket file.
- The dashboard is a second Zetaris query on the same tables the draft cites. That is a design choice and an extra mention of Zetaris.
- Jedi Pack Inc is amber, with a lightsaber mark, on intake, classify, close, send or hold, and the trace.
- Both scope versions stay written until the group rules. The differences are decisions D1–D9 in the brief. Kristof's position is that an H-MEM learn proof ships in whichever version is built: run A writes a memory id, run B reads it and skips a chunk, and a dashboard reject moves the floor.
