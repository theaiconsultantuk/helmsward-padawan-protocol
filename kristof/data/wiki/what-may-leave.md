# What may leave the company

Fictional policy for Jedi Pack Inc. The company is not real.

A reply to a customer may quote the policy limit, the order reference the customer already wrote, and a short answer to their question.

A reply may not include a stored card number, an internal staff price list, or a staff home address.
If the draft would need any of those, may_leave is false and the ticket is held.
